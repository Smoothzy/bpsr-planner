# 🎯 Multi-User Quick Reference Card

## For Raid Leaders

**Create & Share:**
1. Get GitHub token: https://github.com/settings/tokens → "gist" scope
2. Paste token in planner
3. Click "📤 Save to Gist"
4. Share the **Gist ID** (not token!) with your group

**Update Data:**
- Click "📤 Save to Gist" after making changes
- Or enable "🔄 Auto-Sync" to load updates automatically

---

## For Raid Members

**Connect:**
1. Get Gist ID from your raid leader
2. Enter it in the "Gist ID" field
3. Click "📥 Load from Gist"
4. Enable "🔄 Auto-Sync: ON"

**No Token Needed!** You can read/load without a GitHub account.

---

## Quick Buttons

| Button | What It Does | Need Token? |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| 📥 Load from Gist | Download latest player data | ❌ No |
| 📤 Save to Gist | Upload your changes | ✅ Yes |
| 🔄 Auto-Sync | Toggle auto-refresh (30s) | ❌ No |
| ❌ Disconnect | Stop syncing, keep local data | ❌ No |

---

## Status Messages

- ✅ Green = Success
- ❌ Red = Error
- ℹ️ Blue = Info
- ⏳ Yellow = Loading

---

## Common Issues

**"Failed to save: 401"** → Invalid/missing token
**"Failed to load: 404"** → Wrong Gist ID
**Auto-sync not working** → Click the button to turn it ON (should show green)

---

## Pro Tips

- ✅ Enable auto-sync and forget about it
- ✅ Save after updating your availability
- ✅ Keep your token private (only share Gist ID)
- ✅ Works offline - syncs when you're back online
